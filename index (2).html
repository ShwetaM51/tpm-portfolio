How I run programs

Engineering taught me where programs really slip. These are the habits I bring to every one.

Understand the system first

I read the design docs and follow the code paths, so estimates, risks and tradeoffs get real scrutiny.

Make risk visible early

Risks and dependencies live on one list with clear owners, reviewed every week, not discovered at launch.

Keep one source of truth

Roadmap, milestones and status sit in one place, so every team works from the same plan.

Measure what matters

Success metrics are agreed before launch: SLOs for platforms, quality metrics for AI systems.
